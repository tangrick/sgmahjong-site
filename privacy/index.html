Privacy Policy

Updated 31 October 2026

In short: SG Mahjong has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your game data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, unless you share it with Game Center friends, post a win to the Hall of Fame or use Game Center leaderboards.

This policy explains what the SG Mahjong app for iPhone and iPad ("the app"), made by Yew Huat Tang ("we"), does with information. If you have questions, email richardtang@me.com.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask you to create an account or give us your email, phone number or real name.
  • We don't show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, or share data with data brokers.
  • We don't sell or rent any information.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following on your iPhone or iPad. We cannot see it:

  • The player name you type (a nickname of your choice).
  • Your settings (台 limit, stakes, sound and so on).
  • Your play-money bankroll, rank points (LP), hand statistics and the computer players' bankrolls. These are kept in the device's secure storage so they survive reinstalling the app.
  • Your saved big wins (hands worth 5 台 or more).
  • Your LAN History: the LAN games you played, with every hand and how much each player won or lost.
  • Players or posts you have blocked or hidden in the Hall of Fame.

iCloud sync of your bankroll and rank

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app copies your bankroll, rank points and the computer players' bankrolls to your own iCloud key-value storage, so every iPhone and iPad signed in to your Apple ID shows the same, latest record. This storage belongs to your iCloud account and is run by Apple; we cannot see it.

Friends (Game Center friends)

If you are signed in to Game Center and sharing is on (Settings → Friends, on by default), the app stores a "player card" in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database so your Game Center friends can see it on their Friends tab:

  • your player name,
  • your rank, rank points (LP) and play-money bankroll,
  • your 30 most recent big wins (the hands, their 台 and payouts).

The card is saved under your Game Center player ID for this game, which is how your friends' apps find it. To see your friends, the app asks for permission to read your Game Center friends list; it uses the list only to show their cards. Turning sharing off in Settings deletes your card.

Game Center leaderboards

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the app posts your current bankroll and rank points to the "Top Bankroll" and "Top Rank" leaderboards, where other players can see them next to your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app at any time.

Hall of Fame (only if you share)

Nothing is posted unless you tap Share to Hall of Fame and agree. When you do, the app stores the following in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database, where everyone who uses the app can see it:

  • your player name,
  • the winning hand, its 台 breakdown and play-money payout,
  • the date of the win and your rank at the time.

iCloud attaches an anonymous identifier to each post so that only you can remove it and so other players can block it. That identifier is unique to this app and does not reveal your Apple ID, email or name. We can't see who you are from it.

You can remove a post at any time: open it in Big Wins → My Big Wins and tap Remove from Hall of Fame. You can also email us to have a post removed.

Playing on the local network

When you host or join a LAN game, the app talks directly to other iPhones and iPads on the same Wi‑Fi using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity. Your player name, rank, bankroll and game moves are sent to the other players at the table. This data goes directly between devices and is never sent to us.

Reports

If you report a Hall of Fame post, your email app opens a message to us with the post's ID. We receive whatever you send in that email and use it only to review the report.

Children

The app includes simulated gambling with play money and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Deleting the app removes your settings, saved big wins and LAN History. The bankroll and rank are kept in your device's secure storage and your iCloud; to remove them, contact us and we'll explain how.
  • You can stop sharing with Game Center friends in Settings → Friends, which deletes your player card.
  • You can remove your Hall of Fame posts in the app or by emailing us.
  • You can stop leaderboard posting by signing out of Game Center.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
