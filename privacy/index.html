Privacy Policy

Updated 8 November 2026

In short: SG Mahjong has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your game data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, unless you share it with Game Center friends or the people at your table, post a win to the Hall of Fame (on by default, can be turned off) or use Game Center leaderboards.

This policy explains what the SG Mahjong app for iPhone and iPad ("the app"), made by Yew Huat Tang ("we"), does with information. If you have questions, email richardtang@me.com.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask you to create an account or give us your email, phone number or real name.
  • We don't show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, or share data with data brokers.
  • We don't sell or rent any information.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts or microphone. The app only uses a photo if you choose one as your profile photo (see below).

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following on your iPhone or iPad:

  • The player name you type (a nickname of your choice) and your profile photo, if you choose one.
  • Your settings (台 limit, house rules, sound and so on).
  • Your career points, rank points (LP), hand statistics and the computer players' career points. These are kept in the device's secure storage so they survive reinstalling the app.
  • Your saved big wins (hands worth 5 台 or more).
  • Your History: the LAN and online games you played, with every hand and each player's final score.
  • Replays of your last 40 games, as you saw them (your tiles and what was played on the table). You can turn this off in Settings → Friends → Save game replays.
  • Players you muted or blocked, and the Hall of Fame posts you hid.

iCloud sync (your own iCloud)

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps your devices in step through your own iCloud account:

  • your career points, rank points (LP) and the computer players' career points, in your iCloud key-value storage;
  • your History, Replays, Big Wins, a paused single-player game and which Videomarks you've already seen, in your private iCloud (CloudKit) database.

This storage belongs to your iCloud account and is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot see it.

Profile photo

You can choose a profile photo from your photo library, or use your Game Center picture. The app only reads the one photo you pick; it can't see the rest of your library. Your photo is shown on your name badge, sent to the other players at LAN and online tables, and included in your Friends player card (below). If you don't choose one, the app shows the first letter of your name instead.

Friends (Game Center friends)

If you are signed in to Game Center and sharing is on (Settings → Friends, on by default), the app stores a "player card" in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database so your Game Center friends can see it:

  • your player name and profile photo,
  • your rank, rank points (LP) and career points,
  • your hand statistics (hands played and won, your best career points and your biggest loss),
  • your 30 most recent big wins (the hands, their 台 and points),
  • a list of your recent games your friends can watch (see Replays below).

The card is saved under your Game Center player ID for this game, which is how your friends' apps find it. To see your friends, the app asks for permission to read your Game Center friends list; it uses the list only to show their cards. Turning sharing off deletes your card and your shared replays.

Online status (only if you choose)

With Show when I'm online on (Settings → Friends), the app stores a short status record in the public database while the app is open: whether you're playing (Single Player, LAN or Online), a code for the table you're at so friends at the same table show together, and when the status expires (a few minutes). Your Game Center friends see it under Friends Online and can invite you to a game. Closing the app marks you offline.

Replays and watching games

With sharing on, your last 20 games are stored in the public database under your Game Center player ID so your Game Center friends can watch them from your player page. A game shared with friends contains the moves as you saw them and the names and photos of the players at the table, but nobody's career points. You choose who can watch your big wins' hands in Settings → Friends → Who can watch my big wins (Everyone, Friends or Only Me). When anyone can watch, you appear under your Game Center nickname and Game Center photo, and the other players' names and photos are left out. The name and photo you chose in the app are only ever shown to your Game Center friends and the people at your table.

Your Game Center friends can also watch a single-player game while you play it, and ask to take a seat. This goes through Apple Game Center, directly between devices; we don't receive or store it.

Videomarks

You can send a hand from one of your games to Game Center friends with a Videomark (for example "Great play"), and react to Videomarks you receive. Videomarks and reactions use a fixed list of phrases and emoji; you can't type your own text. Each one is stored in the public database with your Game Center player ID, your player name, the friend it's for, the game and hand, and the date, so your friend's app can show it. iCloud sends your friend a notification. The game itself is shared as described under Replays.

Game Center leaderboards

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the app posts your current career points and rank points to the "Career Points" and "Top Rank" leaderboards, where other players can see them next to your Game Center nickname. The app's Leaderboard screen shows these scores with Game Center nicknames and photos; only your Game Center friends see the name and photo you chose in the app. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app at any time.

Hall of Fame

With Post my big wins to the Hall of Fame on (Settings → Friends, on by default), your best winning hand of 5 台 or more is posted to the Hall of Fame by itself, and replaced when you win a bigger one. Turn the switch off and nothing is posted. A post is stored in the public database, where everyone who uses the app can see it:

  • your Game Center nickname and Game Center photo (or "Player" if you aren't signed in to Game Center). The name and photo you chose in the app are not posted,
  • the winning hand, its 台 breakdown and points,
  • the date of the win and your rank at the time,
  • the hand's replay, unless "Who can watch my big wins" is set to Only Me.

When you like a post, the app stores the like with your Game Center player ID and nickname, so the poster sees who liked it.

Every post has Report and Block. Report opens an email to us about the post. Block hides that player's posts and Videomarks on your device.

You can take your post down at any time from Leaderboard → Big Wins → Mine (it isn't posted again), or email us to have a post removed.

Playing on the local network

When you host or join a LAN game, the app talks directly to other iPhones and iPads on the same Wi‑Fi using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity. Your player name, profile photo, rank, career points, game moves and chat messages are sent to the other players at the table. This data goes directly between devices and is never sent to us.

Playing online

Online tables use Apple Game Center. You can only invite your Game Center friends, never strangers. Your player name, profile photo, rank, career points, game moves and chat messages are sent to the other players at the table through Game Center, run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive or store any of it.

Table chat

At LAN and online tables you can send short chat messages to the other players. People watching a friend's game can chat with them the same way. Messages go only to the people at that table, are not saved after the game, and are never sent to us. Messages with offensive words are blocked, and you can mute, block or report any player.

Reports

If you report a Hall of Fame post or a player (in the app or by email), we receive what's in that email, such as the post and the player's nickname and ID, and use it only to review the report.

Children

The app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Deleting the app removes your settings and the copies on that device. Your History, Replays and Big Wins stay in your private iCloud until you delete them in the Settings app (your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → SG Mahjong), or contact us and we will explain how. Your career points and rank are kept in your device's secure storage and your iCloud; to remove them, contact us and we'll explain how.
  • You can stop sharing with Game Center friends in Settings → Friends, which deletes your player card and shared replays. You can also turn off your online status and Hall of Fame posting there.
  • You can change or remove the profile photo you chose at any time by tapping your name badge on the front page.
  • You can take down your Hall of Fame post in the app or by emailing us.
  • You can stop leaderboard posting by signing out of Game Center.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
