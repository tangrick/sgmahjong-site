Privacy Policy

Updated 2 November 2026

In short: SG Mahjong has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your game data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, unless you share it with Game Center friends or the people at your table, post a win to the Hall of Fame or use Game Center leaderboards.

This policy explains what the SG Mahjong app for iPhone and iPad ("the app"), made by Yew Huat Tang ("we"), does with information. If you have questions, email richardtang@me.com.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask you to create an account or give us your email, phone number or real name.
  • We don't show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, or share data with data brokers.
  • We don't sell or rent any information.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts or microphone. The app only uses a photo if you choose one as your profile photo (see below).

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following on your iPhone or iPad. We cannot see it:

  • The player name you type (a nickname of your choice) and your profile photo, if you choose one.
  • Your settings (台 limit, house rules, sound and so on).
  • Your career points, rank points (LP), hand statistics and the computer players' career points. These are kept in the device's secure storage so they survive reinstalling the app.
  • Your saved big wins (hands worth 5 台 or more).
  • Your LAN History: the LAN games you played, with every hand and each player's final score.
  • Players or posts you have blocked or hidden in the Hall of Fame.

iCloud sync of your points and rank

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app copies your career points, rank points (LP) and the computer players' career points to your own iCloud key-value storage, so every iPhone and iPad signed in to your Apple ID shows the same, latest record. This storage belongs to your iCloud account and is run by Apple; we cannot see it.

Profile photo

You can choose a profile photo from your photo library, or use your Game Center picture. The app only reads the one photo you pick; it can't see the rest of your library. Your photo is shown on your name badge, sent to the other players at LAN and online tables, and included in your Friends player card (below). If you don't choose one, the app shows the first letter of your name instead.

Friends (Game Center friends)

If you are signed in to Game Center and sharing is on (Settings → Friends, on by default), the app stores a "player card" in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database so your Game Center friends can see it on their Friends tab:

  • your player name and profile photo,
  • your rank, rank points (LP) and career points,
  • your 30 most recent big wins (the hands, their 台 and points).

The card is saved under your Game Center player ID for this game, which is how your friends' apps find it. To see your friends, the app asks for permission to read your Game Center friends list; it uses the list only to show their cards. Turning sharing off in Settings deletes your card.

Game Center leaderboards

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the app posts your current career points and rank points to the "Career Points" and "Top Rank" leaderboards, where other players can see them next to your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app at any time.

Hall of Fame (only if you share)

Nothing is posted unless you tap Share to Hall of Fame and agree. When you do, the app stores the following in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database, where everyone who uses the app can see it:

  • your player name,
  • the winning hand, its 台 breakdown and points,
  • the date of the win and your rank at the time.

iCloud attaches an anonymous identifier to each post so that only you can remove it and so other players can block it. That identifier is unique to this app and does not reveal your Apple ID, email or name. We can't see who you are from it.

You can remove a post at any time: open it in Big Wins → My Big Wins and tap Remove from Hall of Fame. You can also email us to have a post removed.

Playing on the local network

When you host or join a LAN game, the app talks directly to other iPhones and iPads on the same Wi‑Fi using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity. Your player name, profile photo, rank, career points, game moves and chat messages are sent to the other players at the table. This data goes directly between devices and is never sent to us.

Playing online

Online tables use Apple Game Center. You can only invite your Game Center friends, never strangers. Your player name, profile photo, rank, career points, game moves and chat messages are sent to the other players at the table through Game Center, run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive or store any of it.

Table chat

At LAN and online tables you can send short chat messages to the other players. Messages go only to the people at that table, are not saved after the game, and are never sent to us. Messages with offensive words are blocked, and you can mute any player.

Reports

If you report a Hall of Fame post, your email app opens a message to us with the post's ID. We receive whatever you send in that email and use it only to review the report.

Children

The app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Deleting the app removes your settings, saved big wins and LAN History. Your career points and rank are kept in your device's secure storage and your iCloud; to remove them, contact us and we'll explain how.
  • You can stop sharing with Game Center friends in Settings → Friends, which deletes your player card.
  • You can change or remove the profile photo you chose at any time by tapping your name badge on the front page.
  • You can remove your Hall of Fame posts in the app or by emailing us.
  • You can stop leaderboard posting by signing out of Game Center.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
