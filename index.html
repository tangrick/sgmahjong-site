SINGAPORE MAHJONG FOR iPAD

The Singapore table game you grew up with: animals that bite, flowers, tai and the full payout rules, on a real-looking table with Mandarin calls.

Coming soon to the App Store

Real Singapore rules

148 tiles with cat, rat, rooster and centipede. Instant payouts for 咬到, kongs and flower sets. Tai from dragon pongs to 十三幺, with your choice of tai limit.

Play solo or with friends

Take on three computer players, or host a table for up to four iPads on the same Wi‑Fi. Empty seats are filled by bots.

Bots that get smarter

Climb from 新手 Rookie to 雀聖 Mahjong Master. The higher you rank, the sharper the computer players get: they read the table and stop feeding you wins.

Big wins, remembered

Every hand worth 5 tai or more is saved. Share your best to the public Hall of Fame and compete on Game Center leaderboards.

Sounds like a real table

碰, 吃, 杠, 胡 and the tai count read out in Mandarin, with coins flying to the winner.

Play money only

Stakes and bankroll are for fun. No real money is ever wagered, paid or won, and there are no in-app purchases or ads.
