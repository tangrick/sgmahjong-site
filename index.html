Every tile, every bite, every 台

01 · THE TABLE

A table that feels like the real thing

Ivory tiles slide in from the wall, flip face up and land in the middle with a click. Three computer players sit with you, each with their own face and style.

Three ways to play

Fill the table, 一起玩

Play solo, sit around the same Wi‑Fi, or invite friends from anywhere. Empty seats are always filled by bots.

Single Player

You vs 3 computer players

Bots that get sharper as you climb the ranks, from friendly to ruthless.

  • Ask AI for a hint on your turn
  • Every win counts toward your Career

Local Network

Same Wi‑Fi, up to four players

One iPhone or iPad hosts and the others join. Mix iPhones and iPads at one table.

  • Final scores and places at the end
  • Every game saved in History

Online

Game Center friends, anywhere

Invite your Game Center friends to a private table over the internet.

  • Friends only, never strangers
  • Quick table chat, with mute
Career

Climb from 新手 to 雀聖

Every hand earns or costs career points and League Points (LP). Fill five stars to move up a rank. The higher you go, the smarter the bots get. Your career syncs through iCloud across your iPhone and iPad.

Points are for fun and bragging rights only. They have no cash value and can't be bought.
