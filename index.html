SINGAPORE MAHJONG FOR iPHONE AND iPAD

The Singapore table game you grew up with: animals that bite, flowers, tai and the full scoring rules, on a real-looking table with Mandarin calls.

Coming soon to the App Store

Real Singapore rules

148 tiles with cat, rat, rooster and centipede. Instant bonus points for 咬到, kongs and flower sets. Tai from dragon pongs to 十三幺, with your choice of tai limit.

Three ways to play

Take on three computer players, host a table for up to four iPhones and iPads on the same Wi‑Fi, or invite your Game Center friends to an online table from anywhere. Empty seats are filled by bots.

Chat at the table

Send quick table talk like "Wah, so lucky!" or "快点啦!", or type your own short line. Offensive words are filtered, and you can mute anyone with one tap.

Bots that get smarter

Climb from 新手 Rookie to 雀聖 Mahjong Master. The higher you rank, the sharper the computer players get. Stuck on a hand? Tap Ask AI and Bot Brain tells you what it would play and why, or watch four bots play and learn from their reasoning.

Big wins, remembered

Every hand worth 5 台 or more is saved. See your Game Center friends' ranks and big wins, share your best to the public Hall of Fame and compete on the Career Points and Top Rank leaderboards.

Looks and sounds like a real table

3D ivory tiles that slide, flip and land like the real thing, a choice of table themes, and 碰, 吃, 杠, 胡 and the tai count read out in Mandarin.

No ads, no purchases

Every hand is scored in 台 and points, like any board game. There are no in-app purchases or ads.
