2. Hall of Fame and player names

The Hall of Fame shows players' best winning hands, with their Game Center nickname and photo, to everyone who uses the app. Your best hand of 5 台 or more is posted automatically unless you turn off Post my big wins to the Hall of Fame in Settings › Friends. By leaving posting on you agree that:

  • Your player name and posts contain nothing offensive, hateful, sexual, threatening, harassing or illegal, and don't impersonate anyone.
  • You only post hands you won in the app.
  • There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Offensive player names are hidden automatically. We review reports within 24 hours, remove posts that break these rules, and may stop the players who posted them from posting again.

To report a post or a player, email richardtang@me.com with the player's name and the hand.

3. Table chat and profile photos

Players at a Local Network or online table, and friends watching a game, can chat. Before your first message the app asks you to agree to these rules. The same rules apply to chat messages and profile photos: nothing offensive, hateful, sexual, threatening, harassing or illegal, and the same no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.

  • Messages with offensive words are blocked automatically.
  • Mute a player to hide their messages for the rest of a game.
  • Block a player to hide their messages in every game and ignore their game invites. You can unblock them in Settings › Friends.
  • Report a player from the chat: the app opens an email to us with their name and their messages from that game. We review reports within 24 hours and may stop players who break these rules from using chat or online play.
  • Chat can be switched off in Settings › Friends, and is off when Screen Time doesn't allow talking to other players.

You can also email reports to richardtang@me.com.
