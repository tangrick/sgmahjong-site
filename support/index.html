一Playing

How do I discard?

On your turn, tap a tile to lift it, then tap it again to discard. Drag tiles left or right to arrange your hand.

How do I pong, chow or kong?

When someone discards a tile you can use, buttons appear above your hand. Tap the claim you want, or Pass. If the move clock runs out, a bot makes the move for you.

Where are the rules?

Tap How to Play on the front page. It covers tiles, claiming, 台, limit hands, scoring, ranks and the Hall of Fame. All Tiles shows every one of the 148 tiles with its name.

Can I change the 台 limit or house rules?

Yes. Open Settings on the front page to choose the 台 limit and other house rules before you start a game.

Is money involved?

No. Hands are scored in 台 and points, which have no cash value and can't be bought or cashed out. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad, played in landscape. It needs iOS 17 or later.

二Ranks and career points

How do ranks work?

Winning earns League Points (LP) and paying costs a little. Fill five stars to move up a rank:

新手 Rookie → 銅 Bronze → 銀 Silver → 金 Gold → 玉 Jade → 龍 Dragon → 雀聖 Mahjong Master

Your rank follows your total LP, so losing enough LP drops you back a star or a rank. Computer players get smarter as you climb.

Do LAN and online games count?

Yes. Games against the computer, on your local network and online all add to or take from your career points and LP.

My career points are negative. Can I reset them?

No. Career points only change by winning or losing hands. That keeps the leaderboard fair. You can always keep playing below zero.

My iPhone and iPad show different points

Your career points and rank sync through iCloud, and the device you played on most recently wins.

  1. Sign in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on, on both devices.
  2. Open the app on the device you played on last and wait a few seconds on the front page.
  3. Then open it on the other device.

三Playing with friends

How do I play on the same Wi‑Fi?
  1. Tap Play with Friends → Local Network. All iPhones and iPads must be on the same Wi‑Fi network.
  2. One player taps Host LAN. The others tap Join LAN and choose the table.
  3. The host starts the game. Empty seats get computer players.
I can't see the table on the local network
  • Check that every device is on the same Wi‑Fi network (not a guest network that keeps devices apart).
  • Allow SG Mahjong to find devices on your local network. If you said no, turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.
  • Make sure everyone has the same version of the app.
What happens if someone leaves?

A computer player takes over their seat. If the host leaves, everyone returns to the menu.

Where are my past LAN games?

When a LAN game ends, a result screen shows everyone's final score and place. Every LAN game is saved in History on the front page, with each hand. History is stored only on your device.

How do I play online?
  • Tap Play with Friends → Online Internet and invite your Game Center friends. You need to be signed in to Game Center.
  • Your friends get an invite from Game Center. When they accept, they join your table. Empty seats get computer players.
  • Online tables are friends only: you can't be matched with strangers.

四Chat and profile

How do I chat at the table?

At a LAN or online table, tap the chat button. Pick a quick phrase or type a short message.

How do I stop someone's messages?

In the chat panel, tap the mute button and choose the player. Messages with offensive words are blocked automatically. To report abuse, email us with the player's name.

How do I change my name or photo?

Tap your name badge on the front page. Choose a photo from your library, use your Game Center picture, or remove it.

Your SG Mahjong name and photo are shown to the players at your table and to your Game Center friends. Public screens (Leaderboard → Everyone and the Hall of Fame) show your Game Center nickname and photo instead.

五Leaderboard, Hall of Fame and big wins

Where is the Leaderboard?

Tap Leaderboard on the front page. It has three tabs:

  • Leaderboard: career points and rank from Game Center, for Everyone or just your Friends.
  • Hall of Fame: big hands shared by players.
  • My Big Wins: your own saved hands.
The Leaderboard doesn't load

Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, then open Leaderboard again. Scores can take a few minutes to appear after a game.

How do I see my friends' ranks?

Open Leaderboard and choose Friends. Your friends are your Game Center friends; tap Add Friends in Game Center to invite people. Allow SG Mahjong to see your friends list when asked (or turn it on in Settings → Game Center → Friends List).

How do I stop sharing my rank with friends?

In the app's Settings, turn off Share my rank and big wins with Game Center friends. Your card is removed, so friends no longer see it.

Which hands are saved as big wins?

Every hand you win worth 5 台 or more, or a limit hand, is saved to Leaderboard → My Big Wins, so you can look back at the tiles, the 台 and what you collected.

How do I share a hand to the Hall of Fame?

Open the hand in Leaderboard → My Big Wins and tap Share to Hall of Fame. You need to be signed in to iCloud.

How do I remove my Hall of Fame post?

Open it in Leaderboard → My Big Wins and tap Remove from Hall of Fame.

How do I report a Hall of Fame post?

Posts show only the hand and the player's Game Center nickname and photo. If something looks wrong, email us with the player's name and the hand, and we'll look into it within 24 hours.
