Playing

How do I discard?

On your turn, tap a tile to lift it, then tap it again to discard. Drag tiles left or right to arrange your hand.

Where are the rules?

Tap How to Play on the front page. It covers tiles, claiming, tai, limit hands, scoring, ranks and the Hall of Fame. You can change the tai limit and other house rules in Settings.

Is money involved?

No. Hands are scored in points, which have no cash value. There are no in-app purchases.

Ranks and career points

How do ranks work?

Winning earns League Points (LP) and paying costs a little. Fill five stars to move up a rank, from 新手 Rookie to 雀聖 Mahjong Master. Your rank always follows your total LP, so losing enough LP drops you back a star or a rank. Computer players get smarter as you climb.

My career points are negative. Can I reset them?

No. Career points only change by winning or losing hands. That keeps the leaderboard fair. You can always keep playing below zero.

My iPhone and iPad show different points

Your career points and rank sync through iCloud, and the device you played on most recently wins. Make sure both are signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on, then open the app on the device you played on last and wait a few seconds on the front page before opening it on the other.

Playing with friends (LAN)

  • All iPhones and iPads must be on the same Wi‑Fi network.
  • When asked, allow SG Mahjong to find devices on your local network. If you said no, turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.
  • One player taps Host LAN, the others tap Join LAN and choose the table. The host starts the game; empty seats get computer players.
  • If someone leaves, a computer player takes over their seat. If the host leaves, everyone returns to the menu.
  • When the game ends, a result screen shows how much you won or lost. Every LAN game is saved in LAN History on the front page, with each hand. The "against people" amount leaves out money won from or paid to computer players.

Friends

How do I see my friends' ranks and big wins?

Open Big Wins → Friends. Your friends are your Game Center friends; tap Add Friends in Game Center to invite people. Allow SG Mahjong to see your friends list when asked (or turn it on in Settings → Game Center → Friends List).

How do I stop sharing mine?

Turn off Settings → Friends → Share my rank and big wins. This removes your card so friends no longer see it.

Hall of Fame and leaderboards

How do I share a big win?

Hands worth 5 台 or more are saved to Big Wins. Open one and tap Share to Hall of Fame. You need to be signed in to iCloud.

How do I remove my post?

Open it in Big Wins → My Big Wins and tap Remove from Hall of Fame.

How do I report or block someone?

In the Hall of Fame, open the post and tap Report Post or Block. Reported posts are reviewed within 24 hours. To see blocked players again, use Settings → Unblock all Hall of Fame players.

Leaderboards don't appear

Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, then tap your rank card on the front page.
